Loop Element 01

The Cardboard Box Mechanism

Physical cash dropped into the unkeyed Madrassah box near the gate is collected daily by the elderly handler to fund student transport.

Loop Element 02

Vacation & Surplus Anomaly

The box remains active during school holidays when no transport operates, creating unrecorded cash surpluses managed outside formal ledgers.

Loop Element 03

Out-of-Pocket Top-Ups

When monthly box collections fall short of the Rs 16,900 transport fee, the handler personally covers the difference, blurring institutional transparency.
